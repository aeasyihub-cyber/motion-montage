# Reveal styles: ask the user which one

Ask once, before the storyboard. Offer all four, with these effects.

| Style | What the viewer sees | Good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sync-only** (default) | A word, card or icon appears on its onset and nothing is visible earlier. | Pro, clean edits; voice and picture feel locked | Frames between lines can look sparse, so fill them with motion (camera drift, background ribbons), not text |
| **Ghost karaoke** | Whole line at 13-16% opacity, each word lights as spoken | Full thumbnails, music-led lyric videos | Viewer reads ahead of the voice; reads as "text before sound" |
| **Hero words only** | Only 1-2 key words per line, huge, with a spring pop | Short punchy reels, strong hooks | Less information on screen |
| **Captions bar** | Small lower-third captions that follow the voice | Accessibility, sound-off viewing | Least cinematic |

Also ask: colour mood (dark brand / light "Sky Light" / warm), motion energy (calm, medium, high), camera punch-ins on hero words (yes/no), and whether images and skill cards pop with their word.

## Rules that hold in every style
- No element appears before its word. No text lingers more than ~0.4 s after its line ends.
- Images, icons and cards are tied to the word that names them (use word onsets, not scene starts).
- Do not touch the user's voice: no denoise, no EQ, no loudness change. Only cut mistakes they ask for, with a 20 ms crossfade.
